Rater jon: 300 labels in 2 lists

  1. Agree (correct): the feature is really there at that spot, even in poor condition (faded crosswalk, steep or broken ramp, cobbles). Condition goes in severity and tags, not the vote.
  2. Disagree (incorrect): the feature is absent, somewhere else, or another type (a driveway marked as a curb ramp, a road marked as sidewalk surface).
  3. Unsure: only when the imagery cannot show it.

Mask the votes first: the CSS below in Stylus, or the console snippet on each page.
