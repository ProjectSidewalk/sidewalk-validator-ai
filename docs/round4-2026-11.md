## Why

Production holds back every Disagree map: a validator that can only Agree cannot remove wrong labels, and the error
class this umbrella is about (real but degraded features that the crowd votes wrong) is exactly where the crowd
reference is weakest. The pieces that would let a validator vote Disagree responsibly (confident learning, the
stricter vote rules, the `majority2` prior, the expert gold set and the VLM adjudicator) were scattered over four
studies, none wired to the others, and nothing said in advance what result would let a Disagree map leave hold-back.

Every model so far trained on the gold set's own labels **and on the rest of their scenes** (other train rows on the
same panorama or within the split's 3 m merge). This run makes the gold set usable as a judge: it trains every
candidate without them, scores every candidate on them, and writes the bar down before anything is scored.

This run is the **"round-4 recipe on round-3 data"**: it reuses round 3's splits, crops and confident-learning flags.
It is not a new data pull (J1).

## The arms

- **`round3`** (reference row): round 3's own model and predictions. Its training included the gold rows and their
  scenes; every table that shows it says so.
- **`baseline`** (gold-free): round 3's split with every train row that is a gold item (`gold_item`) or shares a
  scene with one (`gold_scene`: same deployment and panorama, or within 3 m by the split's own transitive merge) set
  to `train_dropped`. Gold items outside the split or in val/test count too (their scenes are found through
  pseudo-rows). Val, test and test_heldout rows are byte-identical to round 3's, so every arm is scored on the same
  rows. Seeds 42, 43, 44.
- **`cl-drop`**: the gold-free base with round 3's confident-learning flags dropped (the flags were computed on
  round 3's split; `round4.py variants` checks the split hash). CurbRamp, Crosswalk, NoCurbRamp and Obstacle; seeds
  42, 43, 44. SurfaceProblem's recipe is the baseline (#76 found no help there).
- **Stage 2 only:** `symmetric-rb` (the `symmetric` rule, then rebalanced to the base's class ratio) and
  `matched-rb` (the size-matched control: as many crowd-sourced `incorrect` rows dropped by a seeded rank that never
  looks at the vote margin, expert-sourced rows spared, then the same `correct` drop); the rank seed is the training
  seed. CurbRamp and Crosswalk.

A gold-scene drop larger than `max(2 x the panorama-only drop, 3% of the type's train rows)` stops `prep` (the 3 m
merge is transitive); Jon decides whether to accept a larger one.

- The crowd reference shares the error class, so a crowd-referenced precision cannot see the mistake #74 is about.
- The gold set can see it, but holds 46 to 79 expert-incorrect items per type, so a gated subset of 15 to 40 can
  contradict a map and cannot certify one.
- The gold set is Disagree-enriched and its strata were sampled at different rates, so a raw expert-confirmed share
  among gated Disagrees is **above** what production would see at a 4 to 25% prior. A3 below is a veto that is
  conservative in one direction only; the pool-weighted share is printed beside it.

So: offline numbers screen, the gold set vetoes, production outcomes plus an expert spot check decide.

### Primary contrasts

Reading rule as in the target scoreboard: an interval that excludes 0 is a difference; one that spans 0 is reported
as what it rules out at each end, never as "no effect". No multiplicity correction; the primaries are counted.

- **P4. `cl-drop` on the error class: a harm screen** (four primaries: CurbRamp, Crosswalk, NoCurbRamp, Obstacle).
  Rows: decided, masked-only gold items of the type whose 2025-08 AI vote was Disagree, with a uniform crop, not a
  round-3 val row. Statistic: round-stratified AUC of `p_incorrect` against the expert (per-round AUCs weighted by
  n_correct x n_incorrect), `cl-drop` minus the gold-free baseline, **the mean of the three per-seed paired deltas**
  (seed s against seed s, same items, one label-level resample shared by every seed and both arms, 2,000 resamples,
  seed 0). **P4 can detect a harm of the size below and cannot confirm the benefit** (#76's held-out effect was
  +0.009 to +0.020). The evidence for adopting `cl-drop` is the held-out replication plus the cost rules C2 and C3.
  Needs stage 2 (uniform gold crops).

### Decision rules

**Recipe per type.** `cl-drop` is the recipe for CurbRamp, Crosswalk, NoCurbRamp and Obstacle unless any of:

- **C1 (harm on gold):** P4's interval lies wholly below 0.
- **C2 (in-distribution cost):** its mean test AUC over three seeds is more than 0.010 under the baseline's.
- **C3 (Agree non-regression):** under `majority2`, with each run's own uncapped map fit on val, on `test` or on
  `test_heldout`: the Agree verdict's prior-weighted coverage at served accuracy 0.92 (seed mean) is more than 0.03
  absolute below the gold-free baseline's, **or** its prior-weighted Agree precision (seed mean) is more than 0.01
  below the baseline's. Whether each arm's Agree precision is under 0.92 is reported as a descriptive column; it does
  not decide C3 by itself, because the map is fit on val and both arms scatter around 0.92 on test.
- **C4 (no replication):** the mean held-out delta is negative with an interval wholly below 0.

SurfaceProblem's recipe is the baseline. C2, C3 and C4 are decidable from the pilot; C1 needs stage 2. Until then the
recipe is reported as "provisional (gold not scored)".

`symmetric` (stage 2): #56's second box is answered "yes, test it on a fresh pull" only if both P5 co-primaries
exclude 0 on the positive side; adopting it then needs a new `data_generation.py` pull with an `--incorrect-rule`
option (#75 step 1), because a half-size model cannot be compared with a full-size one. Otherwise the answer is "no,
the training rule stays `margin`" and #75 closes with that table. Expected, stated in advance: not adopted (P1 found
the dropped two-vote Disagrees confirmed by the expert 68 of 85 times).

**Tier A: a type's Disagree map becomes `shadow_eligible`** when all hold for the type's recipe:

- **A1, test, crowd reference.** Seed-42 model, uncapped map fit on val, `majority2` prior: at least 30 gated
  Disagrees on `test`, prior-weighted precision at least 0.92. The weighted Wilson bound and `raw_precision_needed`
  are printed; the bound is not a criterion (see above).
- **A2, unseen deployments.** With at least 10 gated Disagrees on `test_heldout`, their prior-weighted precision is
  at least 0.92. Fewer than 10: "unseen deployments unestimable", which does not block Tier A and limits Tier B to
  deployments the model trained on.
- **A3, gold, expert reference.** On the type's headline gold population (decided, masked-only, uniform crop, not a
  val row; no gold item or gold scene in training): with 20 or more gated Disagrees, the raw expert-confirmed share
  is at least 0.92 and its Wilson 95% lower bound at least 0.80; with 1 to 19, none is expert-overturned (reported as
  thin); with 0, the gold set is silent, recorded, and does not block. Reported per round as well; a round whose
  gated Disagrees are overturned at twice the other's rate is flagged.
- **Stability.** A1 and A3 also hold for at least one of the two other seeds, each with its own map. Otherwise
  `hold_back (seed-fragile)`.
- A type whose uncapped Disagree map never reaches 0.92 is `hold_back (never clears)`, as today.

`shadow_eligible` means only: the type's uncapped Disagree map is published as a side file next to the capped one,
so would-be gated Disagrees can be replayed over the confidences production records. The served map stays capped.
No vote, no user-visible change. Replacing the served round-3 revisions with round-4 ones restarts
`deployment_gate.py`'s per-revision evidence (J8). `disagree_gate.py decide` cannot emit any status above
`shadow_eligible`.

**Tier B: a Disagree may vote in a deployment** (not decided by this run): for that deployment and type, after the
round-4 model is live with Disagree capped, the deployment-gate rule passes on the replayed gated Disagrees (at least
50 decided under `majority2`, Wilson 95% lower bound at least 0.92, fixed checkpoints); **and** a masked expert
re-label of at least 50 replayed gated Disagrees of that type, drawn to include single-vote and unvoted labels,
confirms at least 0.92 with a lower bound of at least 0.85; **and** the project leads sign off. Nothing in Tier B is
built here.

## What this does not establish

- This run keeps round 3's training rows that were placed by the AI labeller, so it cannot carry #79's held-out
  AI-label slice or its with/without ablation; those belong to the fresh-pull launch (J1).
- Round 4 keeps the current expert-vote rule in the targets and does not answer #74's open box on whether expert
  votes outrank a crowd majority.
- The held-out split was used to pick `cl-drop` (#76), so its held-out delta is a replication check, not a
  confirmation.
- The gold set is a Disagree-enriched stratified sample, not production traffic, with no single-vote or tied
  Disagrees (#106).
- The gold set selects the recipe (as a harm screen, C1) and then vetoes the map (A3), on the same items.
- The fold models behind the reused `cl-drop` flags trained on the gold rows with their crowd targets. No expert
  verdict entered them, and no candidate model sees a gold row or scene.
- Dropping gold rows and scenes changes the baseline (0.4 to 2% of train rows per type), hence a retrained gold-free
  baseline with the same three seeds; the `round3` row shows the size of the shift.
- Models from other streams (backbone comparison, context arms) train on round 3's splits, gold rows and scenes
  included. A model that wants to be judged by Tier A must be trained from `$R4/<Type>/split.csv`.
