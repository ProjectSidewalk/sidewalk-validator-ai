# Does Gemini 3.8 Flash add information to round 3? A stacking check (2026-11)

Generated by `analysis/vlm_stacking.py` from existing score files; no model was called. Methods are in the script's docstring. `incorrect` is the positive class; intervals are 95% percentile intervals from 2,000 resamples (seed 20261115).

**Verdict under the pre-stated rule: NO.** Adding Flash to round 3 (the per-type stack, city-grouped 5-fold CV) changes macro AUC by **+0.0041**, crop bootstrap [+0.0020, +0.0063], city bootstrap [+0.0012, +0.0065]. The crop interval excludes zero and the city interval excludes zero. The point estimate is below the 0.005 bar, and the crop interval contains 0.005, so the data neither establish a gain of 0.005 nor rule one out. The rule was a macro gain of at least 0.005 with a crop-bootstrap interval excluding zero.

**Where the gain is.** SurfaceProblem carries most of it: +0.0122, crop [+0.0048, +0.0194], city [-0.0038, +0.0254]. The city interval includes zero, so the gain is uneven across cities. The other types: CurbRamp +0.0045 [-0.0000, +0.0095]; NoCurbRamp +0.0006 [-0.0009, +0.0022]; Obstacle +0.0005 [-0.0030, +0.0042]; Crosswalk +0.0026 [-0.0016, +0.0083]. The in-sample per-type stack, an optimistic bound, gains +0.0057.

**Change from the first version (commit `2d1c37a`).** That version clipped round 3's `p_incorrect` at 1e-06 before the logit, which tied 2,964 crops in round 3's tails; the stack could re-rank them only by Flash, while the reference was the unclipped round 3. The clipped round-3 logit alone scores -0.0035 macro against round 3, and the first version's stack gain was +0.0022 [-0.0003, +0.0049]. Its Obstacle result, -0.0070, came from the clip, not from Flash: the clip alone costs Obstacle -0.0092 (976 crops tied), and unclipped the Obstacle gain is +0.0005 [-0.0030, +0.0042]. The clip is now 1e-12, which only keeps round 3's exact 1.0 values finite; nothing else in the primary analysis changed. The first version is kept as a sensitivity row (section 5).

**Where the 0.005 bar comes from.** The figure first appears in the Planning repository's options report, `reports/2026-11-ai-module-options.md`, section 3 F: "Expect a small effect. 25%: >= 0.005 AUC." The report says its sections 1-5 were written before the results, but its earliest timestamped copy is Planning commit `9d22e08` (2026-11-15 07:30:59 -0800), about 19 minutes after this analysis was first committed (`2d1c37a`, 07:11:32 -0800). Neither [Planning#37](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/Planning/issues/37) nor [Planning#38](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/Planning/issues/38) states it. Nothing on record shows the bar before the run, so it is treated as **post hoc**. The report also gives 0.005 as a forecast (a 25% chance of a gain at least that large), not as a decision rule; the clause that the crop interval exclude zero appears first in this script.

Crops: 7,572 scored by both legs (Flash 7,572, 0 unscored; round 3 13,144), 40 deployments, all public.
